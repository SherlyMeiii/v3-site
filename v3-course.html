📘 Module Information

Module CodeIS71093C
Module TitleApplied UX Topics
Credits15 credits
LevelPostgraduate
Module ConvenorJulia Donate
TermTerm 2

This module explores applied UX research and design methods through weekly guest talks, seminars, and a portfolio-based assessment.

Week 4: UX of Immersive & Digital Performances by Clemence Debaig

⌄

This week features a guest talk on the UX of immersive and digital performances, exploring interactions, user journeys, and the role of emerging technologies in shaping engaging experiences.
