📘 Module Information

Module CodeIS71092D
Module TitleInteraction Science
Credits15 credits
LevelPostgraduate
Module ConvenorDr Elena Marsh
TermTerm 2

This module studies how people sense, perceive and act through interactive systems — from Wizard-of-Oz prototyping and multimodal sensing to the ethics of ambient and adaptive interfaces.
